### <mark style="color:purple;">**2.**</mark> <mark style="color:purple;"></mark><mark style="color:purple;">Configure Header Mobile Elementor Pro</mark>

**Step 1** - Login to your WordPress Dashboard

**Step 2** - Go to **Templates > Saved Templates > Published > Edit**
